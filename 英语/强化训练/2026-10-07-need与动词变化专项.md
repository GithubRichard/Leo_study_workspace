## 学生试题
### 一、单项选择（1—6）
1. Ben needs ___ his homework before dinner.
   A. finish　B. to finish　C. finishing　D. finished
2. The classroom is dirty. It needs ___.
   A. clean　B. to clean　C. cleaning　D. cleaned
3. My sister ___ need to get up early tomorrow.
   A. don't　B. doesn't　C. isn't　D. aren't
4. ___ Lucy need to bring her notebook?
   A. Do　B. Is　C. Does　D. Has
5. These problems need ___ before Friday.
   A. dealing with　B. to deal with　C. deal with　D. dealt with
6. You needn't ___ all the books today.
   A. taking　B. to take　C. takes　D. take

### 二、用括号内词的适当形式填空（7—14）
每条横线可填一个或多个词；有多种正确形式时写出一种即可。
7. Jack usually ________ (need) ten minutes to walk to school.
8. The windows are dirty. They need ________ (wash).
9. The small boat ________ (sink) in the storm yesterday.
10. Yesterday, Lily ________ (take) a deep breath and opened the door.
11. Tom ________ (break) a cup yesterday.
12. This cup is ________ (break), so we cannot use it.
13. Look! The students are ________ (prepare) for the school show.
14. We are preparing ________ (leave) for the station.

### 三、句型转换（15—18）
保持原意；每空一词。
15. They are getting ready for the match.（改为同义句）
    They are ________ ________ the match.
16. Emma needn't worry about the test.（改为同义句）
    Emma ________ ________ ________ worry about the test.
17. The bike needs repairing.（改为同义句）
    The bike needs ________ ________ ________.
18. David needs to buy some paper.（对“buy some paper”提问）
    What ________ David ________ ________ ________?

### 四、综合应用（19—20）

    ② __________________________________________________
20. 根据中文补全句子，每空一词：

    Lucy is ________ ________ the English test now.

## 家长答案与解析（学生做完后再看）
1. **B**。Ben主动完成作业：need to do。
2. **C**。教室需要“被打扫”：need cleaning；也可表达为need to be cleaned，但不在选项中。
3. **B**。My sister为第三人称单数，否定用doesn't need to。
4. **C**。实义动词need的一般现在时疑问句，Lucy前用Does，后接need原形。
5. **A**。问题需要“被处理”，用need dealing with，介词with不可漏。
6. **D**。情态动词needn't后接动词原形，不加to。
7. **needs**。Jack为第三人称单数；usually提示习惯性动作。
8. **washing / to be washed**。窗户是被清洗的对象，两种形式均可。
9. **sank**。yesterday提示一般过去时：sink—sank—sunk。
10. **took**。Yesterday与opened提示过去时：take—took—taken。
11. **broke**。过去发生的主动动作，用break的过去式broke。
12. **broken**。表示杯子“破了”的状态，用broken；break—broke—broken，broke不能放在此处。
13. **preparing**。Look和are提示正在进行，使用are preparing for。
14. **to leave**。prepare to do sth.表示准备做某事。
15. **preparing; for**。get ready for与prepare for对应，保留进行时。
16. **doesn't; need; to**。needn't与doesn't need to在此同义，不能漏否定。
17. **to; be; repaired**。needs repairing = needs to be repaired。
18. **does; need; to; do**。完整句：What does David need to do? 注意does拼写及助动词后need用原形。
19. **This problem needs dealing with. / This problem needs to be dealt with.** 单数主语用needs；deal—dealt—dealt；两种表达都保留with。
20. **preparing; for; took**。now对应is preparing for；Yesterday对应took。
