## 学生题目

1. So far, Lucy ___ three English books this term.

A. has read　B. had read　C. have read　D. has reading

2. By the time we arrived at the station yesterday, the train ___ already ___.

A. has; left　B. had; leave　C. had; left　D. have; left

3. Mr Li ___ in Shanghai since 2020. He still lives here now.

A. had lived　B. has lived　C. have lived　D. has live

4. Yesterday, Tom could not open the door because he ___ his key before he got home.

A. has lost　B. had lose　C. have lost　D. had lost

5. Up to now, we ___ this film twice.

A. have seen　B. had seen　C. has seen　D. have saw

6. At 5 p.m. yesterday, Amy told me that she ___ all her homework already.

A. has finished　B. had finish　C. had finished　D. have finished

7. It is 10 a.m. now. The train left at 9 a.m. An hour ___ since the train ___.

A. had passed; left　B. has passed; left　C. has passed; leave　D. have passed; left

8. At 10 a.m. yesterday, we reached the station. The train had left at 9 a.m. An hour ___ since the train ___.

A. has passed; left　B. had pass; left　C. had passed; leave　D. had passed; left

## 独立答案与解析区（完成后核对）

### 1. A

A：so far表示截至现在，Lucy是单数，用has read。

B：had read需要过去的参照点，本句以现在为参照。

C：Lucy是第三人称单数，不能用have。

D：完成时需要过去分词，不能用reading。

### 2. C

A：到站是昨天的事，回看更早离开的火车应用过去完成时。

B：had后要用过去分词left。

C：火车离开发生在昨天到站之前，用had left。

D：本句以过去到站为参照，且the train是单数。

### 3. B

A：居住延续到现在，没有过去的参照终点。

B：从2020年持续到现在，单数主语用has lived。

C：Mr Li是单数，要用has。

D：has后需要过去分词lived。

### 4. D

A：叙述以昨天回家为参照，丢钥匙发生在此前。

B：had后需要lose的过去分词lost。

C：既不符合过去参照点，也不符合单数主语。

D：丢钥匙发生在昨天回家之前，用had lost。

### 5. A

A：截至现在看过两次，we用have seen。

B：本句截至现在，没有过去的参照终点。

C：we不能搭配has。

D：see的过去分词是seen，saw是过去式。

### 6. C

A：本句站在昨天五点回看更早的完成，用过去完成时。

B：had后需要过去分词finished。

C：在过去的参照点之前已经完成，用had finished。

D：she不能搭配have，且本句有过去参照点。

### 7. B

A：时间流逝截至现在，用has passed。

B：到现在过去一小时用has passed，过去九点离开用left。

C：九点离开是过去发生的事，要用left。

D：An hour是单数，不能用have。

### 8. D

A：本题以昨天十点为参照，应用had passed。

B：had后需要过去分词passed。

C：since从句里的离开发生在过去，用left。

D：到昨天十点已经过了一小时，用had passed；离开用left。
